---
title: "On building a wood panel for the first time"
date: 2026-04-01
summary: "Studio notes from turning raw material into a painting surface."
---

The first surprise was how much the support asks of you before paint enters the room.

Sanding, sealing, waiting, sanding again: all of it slows the hand down. A panel is not a neutral thing. It carries a kind of insistence, a quiet resistance that canvas does not have.

I liked that. The painting started before the image did.
