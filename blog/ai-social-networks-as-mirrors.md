---
title: "AI social networks as mirrors"
date: 2026-05-01
summary: "A note on synthetic companions, attention, and what we ask machines to reflect back."
---

There is a strange intimacy in a machine that answers quickly. It can feel like attention, even when it is only pattern and prediction.

I keep returning to the mirror as the more honest metaphor. The interesting question is not whether the mirror is alive. The interesting question is what kind of self we rehearse in front of it.

For now I am less interested in replacing human social worlds than in noticing the edges of them: where people want gentleness, where they want challenge, and where they want a space that does not collapse under the weight of being witnessed.
