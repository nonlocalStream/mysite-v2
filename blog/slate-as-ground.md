---
title: "Slate as ground: notes from an experiment"
date: 2026-01-01
summary: "What changes when the support is stone, not canvas or wood."
---

Slate makes oil feel newly physical. The brush does not sink in; it negotiates.

I wanted a surface that would refuse me a little. Something colder, flatter, and less forgiving than wood. What emerged was not just a different texture, but a different tempo.

The painting had to arrive in layers, with more listening between them.
